## 🚫 The Problem With Commercial QR Generators

Most free QR code generator websites online (*qr-code-generator.com, qr monkey, etc.*) have become extortion schemes:
1. They generate **"Dynamic QR Codes"** that redirect through their middleman tracking servers.
2. You print 500 physical restaurant menus, wedding invites, or stickers.
3. **14 days later, they deactivate your code** and show an error page demanding **\$35 to \$50 per month** to reactivate your printed materials.
4. They log visitor IP addresses, GPS coordinates, and device data.

**QR Vault Pro is the permanent antidote.** It encodes the true payload directly into the standard ISO-18004 QR matrix. The QR code points directly to your destination with **no middleman, no cloud tracking, and no expiration dates.**

## ⚡ Features

- **🛡️ 100% Offline & Air-Gapped**: Runs entirely inside your browser's local RAM. Works with Wi-Fi completely turned off.
- **🌐 8 Payload Generators**:
  - **Website URLs** (Direct destination, no redirect)
  - **Wi-Fi Auto-Connect** (Prompts phone to join Wi-Fi with one tap)
  - **vCard 3.0 Contacts** (Saves name, phone, email, company directly to phonebook)
  - **Plain Text / Secret Notes**
  - **Email Send & SMS Auto-Fill**
  - **Phone Direct Dial**
  - **Crypto Addresses** (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana)
- **📐 True Vector SVG Export**: Infinite resolution vectors ready for Figma, Adobe Illustrator, or professional vinyl cutters.
- **🖨️ High-Res PNG (1200px & 2400px)**: Razor-sharp exports for physical printing and billboards.
- **🎨 Custom Styling & Color Gradients**: Linear gradients (Cyberpunk, Emerald Ocean, Royal Sunset) and custom dot geometry.
- **🏷️ Custom Center Icon & Logo**: Add Wi-Fi, Web, Phone, or upload your company logo with automatic shield padding.
- **⚡ Bulk Batch Generator**: Paste 50 URLs or table numbers and download all QR codes in a single organized ZIP archive.
- **🔍 Built-in Offline Scanner**: Drag & drop QR images or scan with your webcam directly on desktop to verify payloads.
- **📄 Printable Table Tent / Wall Flyer**: Instantly formats an A4/Letter sign with *"SCAN TO CONNECT"* ready for `window.print()` or Save as PDF.

## 🚀 How to Run

### Option 1: Live Web App (GitHub Pages)
👉 **[Open QR Vault in your browser](https://willmakesrandomshit.github.io/qr-vault/)**

### Option 2: Windows Desktop App (Zero-Install)
1. Download `offline-qr-vault-windows.zip`.
2. Extract the folder.
3. Double-click `Launch-QR-Vault.bat`. It boots inside a native desktop window with no browser URL bars.

### Option 3: Double-Click Local File
Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox. It is a **100% self-contained single-file HTML app** with all libraries embedded.
